## Notes
- These are free-to-use Creative Commons images, but several require attribution and/or ShareAlike.
- The app keeps farmer-uploaded photos separate as local UUID-named files.
- Demo images are remote because this execution environment cannot download third-party binary assets into the output folder.
- Farmer photo uploads support PNG/JPG/GIF/WEBP up to 10 MB, with mobile camera capture and a preview before submission.
